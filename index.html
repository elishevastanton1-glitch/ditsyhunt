      sample sale 101

      Ditsy Hunt is for people who love a bargain with a bit of personality. Sample sales sell out fast and are scattered across the internet, so every week we gather them in one place. New to sample sales? Here is what they are and how to get the most out of one.

      what's a sample sale?

      A short, limited sale where brands clear out samples, past-season pieces, returns and overstock, often at a big discount. Stock is one-off and sizes sell out, so it's first come, first served.

      do I need to book?

      Often, yes. Many sales run on timed slots, so check the button on each sale. If it's walk-in, we say so.

      can I return things?

      Usually not. Most sales are final, so check each item over before you pay and read the rules on the booking page.

      tips

      • Know your size in that brand. Sizing varies a lot between brands, so check what you usually take before you go.
      • Expect one big shared changing room. Everyone tries things on together, so if you're not comfortable, wear a vest and shorts underneath so you can try things on easily.
      • Make friends. Don't be afraid to ask for help in the changing room, like zipping up a dress. Other than that, it gets feisty in there.
      • Check how you can pay. Some sales take card only, others prefer cash.
      • Travel light. Some sales make you leave coats and bags in a cloakroom.
